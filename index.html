Questions

Does it buy anything or log in to my accounts?

No. It only reads public prices, archived pages and owner reviews, then tells your agent what it found. You decide and you buy.

How is this different from Keepa, CamelCamelCamel or Honey?

Keepa and CamelCamelCamel chart Amazon prices, and buy-smart reads CamelCamelCamel too. On top of that it checks other stores, Shopify shops, archived prices and owner complaints, and gives you a verdict with no affiliate links. Honey tests coupon codes; it doesn't tell you if the price itself is real.

Which stores does it work with?

Amazon (US, UK, Germany and more), Shopify and other direct-to-consumer stores, and live prices from about 40 shops including Walmart and Target. It can also compare an annual software plan, such as Semrush, with paying per use.

Where can it get things wrong?

It can't see Amazon's clip-on coupons or Prime-only lightning deals, so mention those in your prompt. Some stores and brand-new products have no archived prices; it says so instead of guessing. Defect searches lean negative, so it links the exact threads it read.

How does treg make money?

Lookups are billed at cost from your prepaid balance, and treg earns on team plans. No affiliate links and no selling your shopping data. Details are in integrate.md.
