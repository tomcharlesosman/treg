Price charts don't catch used units or broken hinges

Six store tricks that turned up in our Nov 25 checks.

Inflated “list” prices

Discount tags often measure off an inflated “list” price the store rarely charges. Comparing price history reveals the real drop.

Pre-sale price hikes

Sellers raise prices weeks before Black Friday so a modest cut looks massive on sale day (Anker Prime 250W).

Used units in the Buy button

When Amazon runs out, a used third-party listing can take over the default Buy button and lose the warranty (Dyson V15).

Sold-out reseller markups

An “in stock” tag on a popular item often hides a third-party seller charging more than retail (AirPods Pro 2).

Known hardware defects

Price graphs never read buyer complaints. Reddit threads catch recurring product flaws before you buy (Sony XM5 hinges).

Lower prices off Amazon

Browser trackers only watch one store. Scanning competing retailers and Shopify shops catches cheaper new stock elsewhere.

Questions and limits

Does it buy anything or log in to my accounts?

No. It never touches your logins, cart, or payment details. It only reads public store pages, price archives, and Reddit threads, then sends the findings to your agent. You decide and you buy.

How is this different from Keepa, CamelCamelCamel, or Honey?

Keepa and CamelCamelCamel only chart Amazon. /buy-smart reads CamelCamelCamel too, then checks competing retailers (including Shopify shops), Wayback archives, and Reddit defect threads to give you one plain verdict with zero affiliate links. Honey tests coupon codes; it won't tell you the base price was hiked in September.

Which stores does it work with?

Amazon (US, UK, Germany, and more), Shopify and direct-to-consumer brands, plus live prices across major retailers like Target and Walmart. It also checks annual SaaS Black Friday promos (like Semrush or Clay) against paying per call on treg.

Where can it get things wrong?

It can't see Amazon clip-on coupons or Prime-only lightning deals, so mention those in your prompt. Brand-new launches and some small stores have no price archive; it flags that instead of guessing. Reddit threads lean negative, which is why every thread is linked for you to judge.

How does treg make money?

Lookups are billed at cost from your prepaid balance after your $1 free credit, and treg earns on team plans. No affiliate commissions, no sponsored picks, and no selling your shopping history. See full billing details.
